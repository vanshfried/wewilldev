Process

Four steps, no mystery in between.

You don't need to know how the technology works. You need to know what happens next, and when. Here's exactly that.

01

Understand

We start with a conversation — what the idea is, what problem it solves, and what "done" actually looks like for you. No jargon required on your end; if you can explain it in plain language, that's enough.

02

Plan

We scope what actually needs to be built for a first working version, and what can reasonably wait. You get a clear picture of what's included, what it will take, and roughly how long it'll take to get there.

03

Build

Design and development happen together, with regular check-ins so you're seeing progress, not waiting for a big reveal at the end. Testing happens throughout, not as an afterthought.

04

Launch & improve

We ship it, then watch how it's actually used. Most products need a round of small adjustments once real people are using them — we stick around for that.

Why it works

Splitting work this way means you're never surprised — you always know what's being built, why, and what's coming after it.
